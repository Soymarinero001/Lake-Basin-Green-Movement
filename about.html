About us

A community movement protecting and restoring the Lake Victoria basin in Kisumu.

Our mission

We protect and restore the ecosystems of the Lake Basin region, focusing on water quality, waste management and biodiversity. We measure our progress in trees planted, waste collected and people taking part, and we set milestones such as quarterly clean-up drives, annual biodiversity surveys and a five-year reforestation target.

Kisumu faces water pollution, deforestation and the effects of climate change. We believe the people who live around the lake are the ones best placed to protect it.

What we do

  • Community clean-ups

    Regular shoreline and neighbourhood waste collection, every quarter.

  • Tree planting

    Restoring degraded land around the lake basin, with a five-year reforestation target.

  • Awareness campaigns

    Practical lessons on waste sorting and living with a changing climate.

  • Youth conservation clubs

    Schools and universities compete to plant, clean and learn.

  • Working with government

    We work with the county environment office and NEMA to strengthen local rules.

Who we work with

  • Local communities

    Residents of Kisumu and the surrounding lake basin areas.

  • Schools and universities

    Student-led clubs and conservation competitions.

  • Government agencies

    The Kisumu County environment office and NEMA.

  • Partners and supporters

    Conservation organisations, faith-based groups and donors.
