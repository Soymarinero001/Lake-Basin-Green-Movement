What we do

  • Community clean-ups

    Regular shoreline and neighbourhood waste collection, every quarter.

  • Tree planting

    Restoring degraded land around the lake basin, with a five-year reforestation target.

  • Awareness campaigns

    Practical lessons on waste sorting and living with a changing climate.

  • Youth conservation clubs

    Schools and universities compete to plant, clean and learn.

  • Working with government

    We work with the county environment office and NEMA to strengthen local rules.
