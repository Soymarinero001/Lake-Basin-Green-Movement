Support us

You do not have to give money to help. Here are ways individuals, schools and businesses can back our work for Lake Victoria.

Ways to help

  • Volunteer your time

    Join a clean-up or a tree planting day. Sign up here.

  • Donate tree seedlings

    Seedlings of suitable trees for our planting days, from a nursery, school or farm.

  • Donate supplies

    Gloves, sacks, rakes, drinking water and other items we need for clean-ups.

  • Sponsor a drive

    A business or organisation can cover the costs of a clean-up or planting day. We will name you as a partner on our Events page if you agree to it.

  • Offer a venue or transport

    A space for workshops and meetings, or a vehicle to move volunteers and seedlings.

  • Partner with us

    Start a conservation club at your school, or organise a joint drive with your group, church or company.

  • Share your skills

    Photography, first aid, teaching, design, planning: if you can do it, we can probably use it.

  • Spread the word

    Share this website with friends, family and your community. Every new volunteer helps.

Tell us how you would like to help

Send us a short email about what you can offer and we will reply as soon as we can.

A note on money

If you would like to support us financially, email us at lakebasingreenmovement@gmail.com and we will share our official details with you. You can also support a drive directly by providing seedlings, supplies or transport. Please do not send money to any personal number claiming to represent us.

When we do accept support, we will tell you what it was used for.
